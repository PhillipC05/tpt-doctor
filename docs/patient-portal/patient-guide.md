# TPT Doctor Patient Portal — User Guide

## Overview

The TPT Doctor Patient Portal gives you secure access to your healthcare information and communication with your care team. Access your portal from any web browser on your computer, tablet, or phone.

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. You will receive an email invitation from your healthcare provider
2. Click the "Activate Account" link in the email
3. Create a strong password (minimum 12 characters with complexity)
4. Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via:
   - Authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator)
   - SMS text message
   - Security key (FIDO2)
5. Review and sign consent forms electronically

### Logging In
1. Visit your provider's patient portal URL
2. Click "Patient Login"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Complete MFA verification
5. You will be directed to your dashboard

### Resetting Your Password
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your email for a password reset link
4. Follow the link to create a new password

## Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **Upcoming Appointments** — Your next scheduled visits
- **Unread Messages** — Secure messages from your care team
- **Recent Lab Results** — Latest test results available for review
- **Medication Reminders** — Prescription refill reminders

## Appointments

### Viewing Appointments
1. Click "Appointments" in the navigation
2. View all upcoming and past appointments
3. Filter by date range or appointment type
4. Click any appointment for details

### Requesting a New Appointment
1. Click "Request Appointment"
2. Select the reason for your visit
3. Choose preferred date and time
4. Select your preferred provider (optional)
5. Add any notes or special requests
6. Click "Submit Request"
7. Your provider will confirm the appointment

### Cancelling or Rescheduling
1. Find the appointment in your list
2. Click "Cancel" or "Reschedule"
3. For cancellations, provide a reason
4. For rescheduling, select new date/time
5. Confirm the change

## Medical Records

### Viewing Your Records
1. Click "Medical Records" in the navigation
2. Browse by category:
   - Visit Summaries
   - Immunization Records
   - Medication History
   - Problem List
   - Allergy Information
3. Click any record to view full details

### Downloading Records
1. Navigate to the record you want
2. Click "Download" or "Print"
3. Records are available as PDF
4. You may request a complete record export from your provider

## Lab Results

### Viewing Results
1. Click "Lab Results" in the navigation
2. Results are organized by date (newest first)
3. Each result shows:
   - Test name and LOINC code
   - Your result value
   - Reference range
   - Normal/Abnormal indicator
4. Click any result for detailed information

### Understanding Results
- **Normal results** are shown with a green checkmark
- **Abnormal results** are flagged with a yellow warning
- **Critical results** are flagged with a red alert
- Contact your provider if you have questions about results

## Secure Messaging

### Reading Messages
1. Click "Messages" in the navigation
2. Your inbox shows all messages from your care team
3. Unread messages are bolded
4. Click a message to read it and view the thread

### Sending Messages
1. Click "Compose" to send a new message
2. Select the recipient (your provider, nurse, or office staff)
3. Enter a subject (e.g., "Question about medication", "Scheduling question")
4. Write your message
5. Attach files if needed (limit: 25MB per message)
6. Click "Send"

### Message Security
- All messages are encrypted and HIPAA-compliant
- Do not use for emergencies — call 911 or your provider's office
- Messages become part of your medical record
- Providers typically respond within 1-2 business days

## Profile Management

### Updating Your Information
1. Click "Profile" in the navigation
2. You can update:
   - Phone number
   - Email address
   - Mailing address
   - Emergency contact information
3. Click "Save" to apply changes
4. Some changes (like name or DOB) require office verification

### Managing Notifications
1. In Profile settings, click "Notifications"
2. Choose your preferences:
   - Appointment reminders (Email, SMS, or both)
   - New lab results
   - New messages
   - Prescription refill reminders

## Consent Management

### Viewing Your Consents
1. Click "Consents" in the navigation
2. View all consent types:
   - Treatment Consent
   - Payment/Insurance Consent
   - Healthcare Operations
   - Research Participation (optional)
   - Telemedicine Consent
   - Recording Consent
3. Each consent shows its current status (Granted/Revoked)

### Managing Consents
1. Toggle any consent on or off
2. Review the associated privacy notice
3. Adding notes is optional but recommended
4. Click "Save" to update
5. Changes take effect immediately
6. Your provider is notified of consent changes

## Security & Privacy

### Your Rights Under HIPAA
- **Access:** You have the right to view and obtain copies of your health records
- **Amendment:** You can request corrections to your health information
- **Accounting of Disclosures:** You can request a list of who has accessed your PHI
- **Restriction:** You can request restrictions on uses/disclosures of your PHI
- **Confidential Communication:** You can request alternative communication methods
- **Complaint:** You can file a complaint with HHS if you believe your rights were violated

### Protecting Your Account
- Never share your password
- Use MFA every time you log in
- Log out when finished, especially on shared devices
- Report suspicious activity to your provider immediately

## Technical Requirements

- **Browser:** Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
- **Internet:** Broadband connection recommended (5+ Mbps)
- **Cookies:** Must be enabled
- **JavaScript:** Must be enabled
- **For Telemedicine:** Camera and microphone required

## Getting Help

- **Technical Issues:** Contact your provider's IT support
- **Medical Questions:** Send a secure message to your care team
- **Emergencies:** Call 911 immediately
- **Portal Feedback:** Use the "Feedback" link in the portal footer
